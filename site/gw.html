How we project points

Appearance points × chance of playing 60+ minutes; goals × position points; assists × 3; clean-sheet chance × position points; DEFCON chance × 2; plus expected bonus. Goal and assist chances come from goalscorer odds or each player's share of team xG and xA. Saves and cards are not counted.
